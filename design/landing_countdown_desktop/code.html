Core Rule: NO BURGERS ALLOWED.

This is a strict policy. A burger is not a sandwich for the purposes of this championship. Violators will be immediately disqualified, mocked, and banned from future events. Ground meat patties on buns are explicitly forbidden.

What is the Sandwich Championship?

An annual gauntlet where culinary legends and passionate amateurs clash to prove their mastery over ingredients layered between bread.

local_fire_department The Heat

Intense, timed rounds. Competitors must source, prep, and assemble their creations under extreme pressure while navigating surprise ingredient twists.

gavel The Judging

A panel of ruthless, Michelin-starred critics evaluate based on structural integrity, flavor profile synergy, crumb texture, and aesthetic presentation.

emoji_events The Glory

The victor claims the Golden Loaf, a $50,000 grand prize, and eternal bragging rights as the undisputed monarch of the sandwich world.
